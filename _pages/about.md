I’m interested in how the world learns: how people, organizations, fields, and countries learn and innovate. I aim to understand how they attend to information, interact with others, generate ideas, and make decisions. My goal is to develop measures and interventions that make these dynamics visible and expand our capacity for learning and innovation. What pulls me is the moment learning gets rewired by a new idea or technology. This motivates my current focus on AI and how it reshapes individual capabilities and the ways we think and work together.

I’m currently a Research Associate in the [Knowledge Lab](https://knowledgelab.org) at the University of Chicago and a Research Data Scientist at OpenSyllabus. I earned my master’s degree in Computational Social Science at the University of Chicago, where I was advised by Professor [James Evans](https://sociology.uchicago.edu/directory/james-evans) and received the Best Thesis Award. My training spans sociology, economics, and computer science.
